=============================================================
QURAN APPLICATION - CINEMATIC LOOPING VIDEO ENVIRONMENTS
=============================================================

This directory holds the looping HTML5 MP4 nature video backgrounds for the Full-Screen Quran Player.

SUPPORTED VIDEO FILES:
---------------------
1. rain.mp4       -> Realistic falling rain and rainy landscape
2. mountains.mp4  -> Majestic mountain peaks with moving clouds/mist
3. forest.mp4     -> Serene forest with swaying leaves & sunbeams
4. ocean.mp4      -> Rolling ocean waves & moving water
5. fire.mp4       -> Realistic campfire/fireplace with dancing flames
6. night.mp4      -> Peaceful starry night sky & moonlight
7. sunset.mp4     -> Golden sunset with slowly moving clouds

HOW TO REPLACE WITH YOUR OWN VIDEOS:
-----------------------------------
1. Obtain any 720p or 1080p MP4 nature video loop (from Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit, or your own camera).
2. Rename the video to match one of the names above (e.g. "rain.mp4").
3. Drop it directly into this folder: public/environments/
4. The Quran player will immediately and automatically play your video in the background!

RECOMMENDED VIDEO FORMAT:
------------------------
- Format: MP4 (H.264 video codec, AAC or no audio)
- Resolution: 720p (1280x720) or 1080p (1920x1080)
- Duration: 10 - 60 seconds seamless loop
- File size: 5MB - 25MB for optimal mobile loading speed
